Pricing

Last updated: 2026-11-11

SLG is free to play: sign in with Google or GitHub and the whole game — cities, armies, seasons — is yours at no cost. Ingots are an optional extra that buys convenience items only. There is no paywall, no subscription and nothing auto-renews.

US$1.99 = 1,000 Ingots One-time payment · no subscription

Coming soon. The purchase flow is still being finalized. The price above is final and will not change when it launches.

What Ingots buy

  • City Move Order — 30 Ingots. Moves one of your cities to a random free tile.
  • Advanced City Move Order — 50 Ingots. Moves one of your cities to coordinates you choose.

Ingots are only used for convenience items like these. They never expire and are not reset when a season changes. Gift credits are different: they are free, granted every season, and reset to zero when the season ends.

Payment and taxes

Payments will be processed by Creem, acting as Merchant of Record. Depending on where you live, taxes may be added at checkout or already included in the displayed price — the final amount is always shown on Creem's checkout page before you confirm the payment.

Refunds

If you bought Ingots and have not used them, you can request a refund within 7 days of the purchase by writing to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} from the email address linked to your account. Ingots you have already spent are not refundable. The full rules are in the Terms of Service.

元宝能买什么

  • 迁城令 — 30 元宝。把一座城随机迁到一块空野地。
  • 高级迁城令 — 50 元宝。把一座城迁到你指定的坐标。

元宝只用于这类便利道具，不会过期，不随换季清零。礼金不同：礼金是免费资源，每赛季发放， 赛季结束后清零。

付款与税费

付款由 Creem 作为 Merchant of Record（记录商户）处理。按你所在地区的规定，税费可能在结账时加收，也可能已包含在 显示价格中——最终金额始终会在 Creem 结账页、你确认付款之前显示。

退款
