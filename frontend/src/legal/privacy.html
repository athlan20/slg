Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-11

SLG is a free browser strategy game run by an independent developer. This page explains, in plain language, what we collect when you play, why we need it, and what you can ask us to do with it.

1. What we collect

  • Sign-in information. If you sign in with Google or GitHub, we receive your email address, your display name or nickname and an account identifier from that provider. We never receive the password for those accounts.
  • Game data. Your cities, resources, buildings, troops, marches, messages and in-game event history, and the agent tokens you create so an AI client can play for you.
  • Technical data. IP address, browser user agent, request time and the pages or API calls you make. We keep this as server logs to run the service, diagnose failures and stop abuse.
  • Local storage in your browser. A session token so you stay signed in, your language and theme choices, and the name of the last account you used. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

2. How we use it

  • To run the game and keep your account working: sign-in, saving your empire, seasons, rankings.
  • To protect the service: rate limiting, cheat and abuse detection, security investigations.
  • To answer your support, refund and account-deletion requests.
  • To see rough usage numbers and decide what to build next.

We do not use your data for advertising or profiling.

3. What we never do

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising or marketing purposes.

4. Third parties

  • Google and GitHub: optional sign-in providers. If you use them, the sign-in happens on their side under their own privacy policies.
  • Creem: our payment platform and Merchant of Record for Ingot purchases. Card numbers and billing details are entered on Creem's checkout and handled by Creem — we never see or store your card details. We only receive confirmation of the order and the amount paid.
  • Hosting and infrastructure providers that run the servers and store the database on our behalf.

These providers only get the data they need to provide the service.

5. Retention and deletion

Account and game data is kept while your account exists. Server logs are kept for a limited period and then discarded.

You can have your account deleted at any time by writing to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} from the email address linked to the account (or by telling us which sign-in provider you used). We then delete the account and its game data. Two exceptions: we may keep basic purchase records for as long as tax and accounting rules require, and we may keep what is needed to resolve an open dispute or abuse case.

6. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age where you live). If you believe a child has created an account, write to us and we will delete it.

7. Your choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, or to object to how it is processed. Write to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} and we will handle it. You can also clear your browser's storage at any time to remove the saved session from that device.

8. Security

Traffic is served over HTTPS and database access is limited to the developer. No service is perfectly secure, but we keep the surface small: we ask you for no password of our own and store no payment details.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes we update the date at the top of this page, and we announce material changes in game.

10. Contact

Privacy questions or deletion requests: {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}. See also the Terms of Service and Pricing.

隐私政策

最后更新：2026-11-11

SLG 是一款由个人开发者运营的免费网页策略游戏。本页用直白的话说明：你玩游戏时我们会收集什么、为什么需要这些 信息，以及你可以要求我们做什么。

一、我们收集什么

  • 登录信息。 使用 Google 或 GitHub 登录时，我们会拿到你的邮箱地址、昵称以及该平台提供的账号 标识。我们不会拿到这些账号的密码。
  • 游戏数据。 你的城池、资源、建筑、军队、行军、聊天与游戏内事件记录，以及你为让 AI 客户端 代玩而生成的 Agent 令牌。
  • 技术数据。 IP 地址、浏览器标识、访问时间与请求的页面或接口。这些作为访问日志保留，用于 运行服务、排查故障和阻止滥用。
  • 浏览器本地存储。 用于保持登录的会话令牌、语言与皮肤选择、上次使用的账号名。我们不使用广告 或跨站跟踪 Cookie。

二、我们怎么用

  • 运行游戏、保证账号正常使用：登录、存档、赛季与排行。
  • 保护服务：限流、外挂与滥用检测、安全问题排查。
  • 回复你的客服、退款与删除账号请求。
  • 了解大致的使用量，决定接下来做什么功能。

我们不会把你的数据用于广告投放或用户画像。

三、我们不会做的事

我们不出售你的个人信息，也不会为广告或营销目的向他人提供。

四、第三方

  • Google 与 GitHub：可选的登录服务商。使用它们的登录在其一侧完成，受其 各自的隐私政策约束。
  • Creem：元宝充值的支付平台与 Merchant of Record（记录商户）。银行卡号与账单信息在 Creem 的结账页填写并由 Creem 处理——我们看不到也不存储你的银行卡信息，只会收到订单 确认与支付金额。
  • 托管与基础设施服务商：代我们运行服务器、存储数据库。

这些服务商只拿到为其服务所必需的数据。

五、保留与删除

账号与游戏数据在账号存在期间保留；访问日志保留有限时间后删除。

你随时可以发邮件到 {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} 申请删除账号（请用与账号绑定的 邮箱发送，或说明你使用的登录方式）。我们会删除账号及其游戏数据。两个例外：购买记录可能按税务与会计要求保留 一段时间；正在处理的争议或滥用事件所需的数据可能保留。

六、儿童

本游戏不面向 13 岁以下儿童（或你所在地区规定的最低年龄）。如果你认为有儿童注册了账号，请联系我们，我们会 删除该账号。

七、你的选择

按你所在地区的规定，你可能有权查阅、更正、导出或删除自己的个人数据，或反对某些处理方式。发邮件到 {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} 即可，我们来处理。你也可以随时清除浏览器存储， 移除该设备上保存的登录状态。

八、安全

访问全程使用 HTTPS，数据库访问仅限开发者本人。没有服务能做到绝对安全，但我们把面收得很小：不要求你设置我们 自己的密码，也不存储任何支付信息。

九、政策更新

本政策如有修改，会更新页面顶部的日期；重要变更会在游戏内公告。

十、联系方式

隐私问题或删除账号申请：{{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}。另见 服务条款 与 定价说明。
