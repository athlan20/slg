2. Fair play

The game is meant to be fun for everyone. Do not:

  • use cheats, bots, scripts, modified clients, exploits or bugs to gain an advantage;

  • You are responsible for every action taken with your token, including actions by a third-party AI client you connect. If a token leaks, revoke it in game immediately; we are not responsible for losses caused by a leaked token.

4. Virtual items and currency

Ingots (元宝) are the game's virtual currency. When purchases are live you will be able to buy them with real money, and they exist only inside this game.

  • Ingots can only be used inside the game, to buy convenience items such as city move orders.
  • Ingots cannot be transferred, gifted, traded or sold to anyone else, cannot be withdrawn, cannot be exchanged for cash or any other currency, and are not connected to any other game or platform.
  • Ingots never expire and are not reset when a season changes.
  • Gift credits are a free resource granted each season. They are reset to zero when the season ends, and they have no cash value.
  • Item prices in Ingots may change over time. The price shown in the shop at the moment you buy is the one that applies.

5. Purchases and refunds

  • Current pricing: US$1.99 for 1,000 Ingots, a one-time payment with no subscription. Details are on the pricing page.
  • Payments are processed by Creem acting as Merchant of Record. Taxes and the final amount are shown on the checkout page before you pay.
  • Refunds: if you have bought Ingots and have not used them, you can request a refund within 7 days of the purchase by writing to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} from the email address linked to your account. Ingots you have already spent are not refundable.
  • Gift credits are free and never refundable.

6. Changes and shutdown

The game is a work in progress: rules, numbers, features and content may change, and the service may be interrupted for maintenance or investigation.

We may suspend or end the game. If we permanently shut the game down, we refund unused purchased Ingots at the price you paid, using the original payment method where possible.

The game is provided "as is" and "as available", without warranties of any kind. We do not promise that it will be uninterrupted or error free, or that saved state is never lost (we do keep backups). To the extent the law allows, we are not liable for indirect or consequential losses, and our total liability is limited to what you paid us in the 12 months before the claim.
