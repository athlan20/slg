**Alliances are here!** Team up with other players and take on the world together.

**1. Creating and joining**

- Reach Town Hall level 5 and pay 100,000 gold to found an alliance. Names are 2–8 characters and must be unique across the server.
- An account can belong to only one alliance, and each alliance holds up to 30 members.
- You can apply to join, or accept an invitation from a leader or deputy. Applications and invitations expire after 48 hours if nobody responds.
- Ranks are Leader (1), Deputy (up to 2) and Member. The leader can appoint or dismiss deputies, hand over leadership, edit the notice, kick anyone and disband the alliance. Deputies can approve applications, invite players, edit the notice and kick regular members.
- After leaving, being kicked or disbanding, a player cannot found or join an alliance for 24 hours. When an alliance is disbanded, only the player who disbanded it is subject to this.
- If a leader is inactive for 7 days, leadership passes automatically to the most recently active deputy, or to the earliest-joined member if there is no deputy.

**2. No attacks between allies**

- You cannot send troops against an ally's cities or wilderness (plunder, capture a branch city, seize wilderness) and cannot scout them. You'll see "Allies cannot be attacked."
- Troops that set out before the target joined your alliance won't fight on arrival; they turn back instead. Transport, reinforcement and troop movement are unaffected.
- Allied territory is colored separately on the main map and shown as green dots on the minimap.

**3. Alliance channel**

- A new "Alliance" tab in chat, visible only to members, and only showing messages from after you joined. Rules match the world channel (one message per 10 seconds, word filter applies).
- System messages appear in the channel when someone joins, leaves, is kicked, or leadership changes.

**4. Map markers**

- Members can mark Rally, Enemy, Resource and Danger locations on the map, with an optional note of up to 20 characters.
- The leader and deputies can remove other members' markers.

**5. Alliance leaderboard and Hall of Fame**

- The leaderboard gains an "Alliance" board showing the top 50. Alliance power = wilderness tiles held by members + famous cities × 10 + the sum of members' capital Town Hall levels. Alliances with fewer than 5 members are not ranked.
- The alliance detail page shows its power and rank.
- At the end of each season, the top three alliances are recorded in the Hall of Fame.
- Alliances and memberships carry over between seasons; applications, invitations and map markers are cleared at season change.

**Notes**

- Alliance management (founding, inviting, kicking, etc.) is for players only; Agents cannot do it on your behalf. Agents can look up alliances, read the alliance channel, and read and write map markers.
- The numbers above (founding cost, member cap, power weights, etc.) are initial settings and may be adjusted later.

Find some reliable partners and go claim those famous cities together.
