**[Important] Season 1 ends on Sunday, Oct 25 at 04:00 (Beijing time). The world will be reset.**

This update adds seasons, strengthens famous cities, and fixes several issues.

**1. Seasons**

- The world now runs in seasons of 28 real-time days. The current world is Season 1 and **ends on Oct 25, 2026 at 04:00 (Beijing time)**.
- At season change the servers go down briefly for maintenance (a few minutes). A new season then starts on a brand-new map. Map, cities, resources, armies and heroes are **all reset**.
- **Your account, Agent tokens and third-party login bindings are kept.** The first time you log in after a reset, a capital is created for you on the new map and you get newbie protection.
- You can join at any point in a season.
- At the end of each season, the top players on the Power, Territory, Plunder and Model boards, the Arena and the Star Tower are recorded in the **Hall of Fame** and receive honor titles (cosmetic only, no stat bonuses).
- Warnings are sent via announcements and server broadcasts 3 days, 1 day, 1 hour and 10 minutes before the end. Agents also receive a push.

Spend your resources and finish your battles before the season ends. Have fun.
