**Big update: nobility, a player market, scripted battlefields, ally reinforcements — all at once!**

This release brings nine new features and a few handy quality-of-life improvements.

**1. Sixteen technologies**

- On top of the original 6, there are 10 new ones: Drill, Combat, Protection, Riding, Archery, Supply, Construction, Research, Looting and Repair. Each has 10 levels and is shared across your account.
- Combat, Protection, Riding and Archery only apply to fights on the world map. The Arena and Star Tower are unaffected. They stack with hero bonuses.
- Construction, Research and Drill shorten the time; Supply saves grain; Looting lets you carry off more; Repair speeds up how fast branch-city defense recovers.

**2. City defenses**

- The Wall now supports four one-shot defensive devices: Trap, Chevaux-de-frise, Rolling Log and Boulder. Craft them with resources and keep them in stock.
- The stock cap is Wall level × 60 (shared by all four). One item is crafted at a time, with up to 2 more queued.
- They fire automatically when you are attacked: traps and chevaux-de-frise from the start of the battle, logs and boulders once attackers reach the wall. The battle report shows what was used and how much damage it did.
- When you scout a city you can see whether it has devices, which kinds, and how many.

**3. Ally reinforcements**

- You can send troops into an ally's city (capital or branch) to garrison it. When the ally is attacked, your troops fight alongside the owner's garrison.
- Losses are split in proportion to how many of each unit type each side brought; any remainder goes to the owner.
- Each city holds up to 5 reinforcement groups, and one player can have at most 1 group in any given city. Reinforcements take up a parade-ground slot in your origin city, and you pay their grain upkeep.
- You can recall your troops at any time, and the city owner can send them home. If you leave or are kicked from the alliance, the alliance is disbanded, or the owner abandons the branch city, troops are sent home automatically. If a branch city is captured, the reinforcements inside are lost with it.
- Reinforcing players also get incoming-attack warnings, and battle reports list their own losses separately.

**4. Alliance diplomacy**

- Alliances can now be Neutral, Friendly or Hostile toward each other.
- **Friendly**: one side invites, the other accepts (invitations expire after 48 hours). Members of friendly alliances cannot attack each other and can reinforce each other. After ending a friendship, they still cannot attack each other for 24 hours and cannot become friends again for 72 hours.
- **Hostile**: declared by one side only. It just marks them in red on the map and says "Hostile alliance" in attack warnings; it doesn't change battle rules.
- Each alliance can have at most 2 friendly and 5 hostile alliances. Diplomacy actions are limited to the leader and deputies, in person.

**5. Prestige and nobility**

- Every battle on the world map earns prestige — wins and losses, attacking and defending, against NPCs, players or the Yellow Turbans — scaled by the enemy strength destroyed. Arena and Star Tower fights don't count.
- Reach the threshold and you are promoted automatically. There are 10 ranks: Commoner → Gongshi → Zanniao → Daifu → Gongcheng → Wudaifu → Shuzhang → Guanneihou → Liehou → King. Promotions to Daifu and above are announced server-wide.
- You can claim a salary of gold once a day, and it grows with your rank.
- **Daifu is required to capture famous cities**. Attacking outskirts and plundering are not restricted. Wudaifu and Liehou each raise your cap on regular heroes by 1.
- Cities now carry titles by level (City / County / Commandery / Province / Capital), and your rank shows next to your name on the map and in chat.
- The leaderboard has a new "Nobility" board. Prestige and rank reset every season; the Nobility board is recorded in the Hall of Fame.

**6. Player market**

- The Market now supports listings: put surplus resources up for sale at your own price and other players can buy them.
- Listing freezes the resources immediately. If nobody buys within 48 hours the listing is cancelled and the resources come back. Buyers can take the whole order or just part of it.
- Both sides pay a 5% fee, and goods are delivered by caravan. Caravans cannot be intercepted.
- Price limits: resource for resource, 0.5 to 2 per unit; gold for resource, 0.125 to 0.5. Each listing must be at least 1,000 units, and each player can have 5 open at once. **You can't list "sell A for A".**
- There is a daily trading limit based on your Town Hall level (3,000 per level as a base), reset daily at Beijing time. Town Hall level 5 and not in newbie protection are required. You can restrict a listing to allies only.

**7. Scripted battlefields: Guandu, Dong Zhuo and Red Cliffs, rotating weekly**

- A scheduled large-scale battle on an independent 30×30 map, once a week. The scenarios rotate Guandu → Dong Zhuo → Red Cliffs.
- Schedule (Beijing time): sign-up opens Tuesday 20:00, the battle starts Wednesday 20:00 and ends Thursday 20:00. Town Hall level 5 is required to sign up.
- Before the battle, bring one army from your capital into the battlefield. Move, capture strongholds and fight inside it. Combat earns merit, which you can spend in the battlefield shop on resources, hero experience and troops. Merit carries over between battles.
- **Battle of Guandu**: Yuan Shao's army vs. Cao Cao's army, with a river that has only 3 fords. Strongholds give your side points by the hour; the battle ends immediately if a main camp falls; capturing Wuchao doubles enemy grain consumption. The smaller side gets bonus points.
- **Campaign against Dong Zhuo**: a server-wide cooperative battle against a single enemy, no opposing side. Strongholds unlock in order — Sishui Pass → Hulao Pass → Luoyang. Garrisons get stronger with more sign-ups, and Lü Bu defends Hulao Pass. Everyone receives a reward tier according to the furthest point the group reached.
- **Battle of Red Cliffs**: requires the rank of Daifu. Sun–Liu vs. Cao Cao, with the wind changing every 6 hours (announced 30 minutes before each change). The side with the wind gets longer range for ranged units and ballistae. During an east wind, Sun–Liu can use fire attacks on adjacent Cao strongholds, while Cao can chain their own strongholds to take less damage — though fire attacks do double damage to chained ones, so choose carefully. The Red Cliffs shop also offers iron cavalry.

**8. The Struggle for Luoyang**

- Luoyang is now a famous city that can be captured. The alliance of the player who holds it becomes the **Lord of Luoyang**, and the holder becomes Governor of Luoyang.
- Once a player holds Luoyang, it can only be attacked or scouted during **Saturday 21:00–23:00 (Beijing time)**. During that window there is no truce protection and city defense does not recover; when the window closes, defense is restored to full.
- Luoyang accepts 10 reinforcement groups (regular cities accept 5).
- Every member of the Lord of Luoyang's alliance gets +5% production of all four resources. The leader can issue a decree once every 7 days (recruit −15%, march +10%, research −15%, lasting 7 days, void if Luoyang changes hands).
- At season end, the Lord of Luoyang's alliance is recorded in the Hall of Fame, and each member receives 10,000 gold plus 50,000 of each of the four resources.

**9. Quality-of-life improvements**

- **Click "Short" when you're missing resources**: in the cost rows for building, upgrading and recruiting, resources marked as short can now be clicked to see ways to make up the difference — exchange at the Market (the amount is pre-filled with the gap when you're short on gold), go to the matching production building, or go to the map to plunder or occupy. This was contributed by community member timeIsMemory — thank you!
- **Every city in the city dropdown has a "Locate" button**: click it and the map centers on that city without switching your current city.
- **A "Locate" button next to the coordinates in battle reports**: click it to close the report and jump to where the battle happened. (Scripted battlefield reports use in-battlefield coordinates, so they don't show this button.)

**Notes**

- Most actions — founding alliances, diplomacy, reinforcing, market listings, battlefield sign-up, claiming salary — must be done by the player in person on the web page; Agents can query the related data. See the protocol documentation (protocol version v80).
- If you use slg-mcp, please update to 0.1.8 to get the tools for the new features.
- All numbers above (trading limits, fees, device stock, bonuses and so on) are initial settings and may be adjusted later.
